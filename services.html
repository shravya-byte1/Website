Pranic Healing is a no-touch, no-drug energy-based practice using prana — life force — to calm the mind, heal the body and harmonize your life. Practitioners cleanse and re-energise chakras in-person or remotely.

01 · Mental & emotional health

Mental Health

This Pranic Healing approach focuses on the energetic dimension of emotional and mental strain. Sessions use Pranic Healing techniques to identify, cleanse and energise areas of the aura and chakras associated with stress, emotional heaviness and mental fatigue, supporting greater calm, clarity and energetic balance.

This path pairs well with the weekly Meditation on the Twin Hearts and Mass Pranic Healing sessions, both built around stress reduction and emotional balance.

02 · Financial abundance

Financial Abundance

Within the Pranic Healing tradition, this work explores how thoughts, emotions and energetic patterns can influence one's relationship with prosperity and abundance. Pranic Healing techniques are used to cleanse and balance the energy associated with limiting thought patterns, emotional blocks and lack of clarity, supporting a more focused and constructive inner state.

The work may include Pranic Healing-based practices such as cleansing and energising, blessing and gratitude practices, meditation, and working with thought-forms to cultivate greater clarity, intention and energetic alignment.

03 · Physical well-being

Physical Well-Being

Pranic Healing works with the energy body to support the body's natural healing process. Sessions involve scanning for areas of congested or depleted energy, cleansing the affected energy field and energising it with fresh prana, with the intention of promoting greater energetic balance and vitality.

This is never presented as a replacement for medical treatment. It's offered as a gentle complementary layer, and anyone with an ongoing condition should keep working with their doctor.
