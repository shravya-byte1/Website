Group Meditation on the Twin Hearts & Free Pranic Healing

One of the best-known techniques in the Pranic Healing system, Meditation on the Twin Hearts is a powerful spiritual practice developed by Grandmaster Choa Kok Sui. It combines activation of the heart and crown energy centres with the practice of blessing the earth with loving-kindness, peace and goodwill — a short guided session that many practitioners describe as deeply peaceful and quietly joyful.

Benefits

What this practice offers

  • Greater inner calmHelps settle mental activity and create a deeper sense of stillness and relaxation.

  • Emotional renewalCreates space to release accumulated emotional heaviness and reconnect with greater balance and lightness.

  • Mental clarityA regular meditation practice can help create greater awareness and a calmer, more centred state of mind.

  • Cultivating loving-kindnessThe practice consciously develops qualities such as compassion, kindness, forgiveness and goodwill.

  • Energy and vitalityThe meditation is traditionally practised to cleanse, energise and harmonise the subtle energy system.

  • A sense of connectionThe blessing component expands the practice beyond oneself, encouraging a feeling of interconnectedness and collective wellbeing.

  • A dedicated moment for reflectionThis weekly gathering provides a recurring opportunity to pause, reflect on what needs to be released, and consciously set an intention for the days ahead.

  • Deeper experience through communityPractising with others can bring a greater sense of commitment, shared intention and belonging than practising alone.

Good to know — This meditation is generally recommended from around age 16, with children 10–15 able to join around once a week. If you have a heart condition, unregulated high blood pressure, glaucoma, or a serious kidney or liver condition, please speak with a practitioner before joining, as the practice may need to be adapted for you.

What to expect

How the session flows

Arrival & settling in

A few quiet moments to arrive, settle into the space and transition away from the pace of daily life.

Introduction to the practice

A brief explanation of Meditation on the Twin Hearts and how to approach the meditation.

Preparation & relaxation

Simple guided preparation to relax the body, calm the mind and become more aware of the energy within.

Meditation on the Twin Hearts

A guided meditation involving the activation of the heart and crown centres, followed by the practice of blessing the earth with loving-kindness, peace and goodwill.

Silence & integration

A period of quiet stillness after the meditation, allowing participants to gently absorb and integrate the experience.

Closing & grounding

The session concludes with grounding and a gradual return to normal awareness.

Community connection

An optional opportunity to connect with fellow participants and share the experience of practising together.
