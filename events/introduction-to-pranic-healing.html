Introduction to Pranic Healing & Its Applications

We care for our physical body — resting when tired, eating when hungry, seeking support when something feels wrong. But there's another dimension of ourselves that often goes unnoticed: our energy. Pranic Healing is a system of energy-based practices that works with the body's energy field through cleansing, energising and balancing. This session is a beginner-friendly, no-touch walkthrough of the aura, the chakras and prana, with each week leaning into a different area of life — physical health, mental health, relationships, financial abundance, or spiritual growth — so the ideas always land somewhere practical. You don't need to know anything beforehand; just come with curiosity.

What to expect

How the session flows

Welcome & introduction

Begin with an overview of Pranic Healing, its origins and the principles behind working with energy.

Understanding energy

Explore the basic concepts of the energy body, prana and how energetic states are understood within Pranic Healing.

How Pranic Healing works

Discover the three fundamental approaches of cleansing, energising and balancing, and how they form the foundation of the practice.

Understanding its applications

Explore how Pranic Healing can be applied across different areas of life, including physical wellbeing, emotional balance, mental clarity, relationships and personal growth — that week's chosen theme.

Live demonstration

Witness a live sample healing to see how Pranic Healing is applied in practice, rather than simply hearing about the technique.

Reflection & experience

Take a moment to notice your own experience and reflect on what you may have discovered or felt during the session.

Exploring the path forward

Understand how Pranic Healing can be explored further, including learning practices for personal self-care and deeper study.

Questions & closing

An open Q&A to clarify what you've experienced and decide if Pranic Healing may be right for you — bring any question about energy, healing or the wider practice, not just tonight's topic, and we'll do our best to address it.
